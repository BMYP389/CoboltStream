How Hosting Works

This player runs 100% in your browser. Local audio files you upload here play immediately during this session.

1 Upload your MP3/WAV files to free object storage like Cloudflare R2, AWS S3, or a Netlify/Vercel static folder.
2 Paste each public https:// audio link into External Audio URL.
3 Export your catalog.json in Share & Publish and deploy this single HTML file so anyone can stream your music.
